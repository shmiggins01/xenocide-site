Xenocide: Swarm Defense
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version: the game does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives any data from your device.

What the game stores

Your campaign progress (worlds cleared, stars earned, unlocked units) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Apple services

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game may use Apple's iCloud to keep that same save data in sync between your own devices. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game may use it for achievements and leaderboards. Both are Apple services that you control in your device's Settings. The data is stored in your own Apple account, is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer cannot see your iCloud data or identify you from it. A leaderboard shows your Game Center nickname and score to other players, as with any Game Center game. You can play the whole game without either service.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics or tracking code.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has none.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game removes its save data from your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
