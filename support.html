Support

Found a bug (the wrong kind), or have a question or a suggestion? Email shmiggins@live.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I build a unit? Tap open ground to bring up the build bar, tap a unit to see its range, then tap it again to deploy. Tap a deployed unit to promote or sell it.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration? Open the menu button at the top right during a mission, or Settings on the title screen.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes its save.

Does the game need an internet connection? No.
